Formula Help

Say the calculation in plain words. You get a working formula for Excel or Google Sheets, with a short explanation of what each part does. Pick an example:

=SUMIFS(C:C, A:A, "Ana", B:B, "October")

In plain words: Add the amounts in column C, but only on rows where column A says Ana and column B says October. We test it on your sheet before it stays.
